# Editorial photography

Feature-block photography for `/solutions` and `/process`. One image per page at
most — these pages are carried by copy and by the stat strips, and a decorative
image in every block would be thirteen more files to keep current.

All six are Unsplash, downloaded and re-encoded to AVIF at 1600px wide rather
than hotlinked: the Unsplash CDN is not a dependency this site should take, and
the rest of the site's photography is self-hosted AVIF.

The Unsplash License permits commercial use without attribution. Photo IDs are
recorded here anyway so a future editor can find the original or its licence.
